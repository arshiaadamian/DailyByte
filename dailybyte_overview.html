What's a REST API?
It's a set of rules that lets your phone ask a server for data — tap to read the 60-second version.
The gap

Most learning apps want 15 minutes.
Most free time is 2.

Courses and book-summary apps assume you'll sit down and commit. But real learning happens in the gaps — waiting for coffee, standing in line — and nothing meets people there with one specific, well-written idea on the exact topic they actually chose.
How it works

Four steps, once a day

01

Pick your topics

Choose what you want to get sharper at — HTTP, AWS, cooking, anything on the list.

02

Get a daily nudge

Once a day, a notification arrives with one small, specific idea from your topic.

03

Read it in a minute

Tap it open: a short, plain-English explanation, freshly written each day.

04

Go deeper if you want

A link to a real source sits right there, for whenever two minutes turns into twenty.

MVP scope

What actually ships first

Ships in v1

  • Sign in with email or Apple ID
  • Pick 1–3 topics from a starting list
  • One daily notification per topic
  • A short written explanation for each concept
  • A link to a real source to learn more
  • A simple history of past days
  • Change your topics or notification time anytime

Comes later, on purpose

  • Quizzes and spaced-repetition review
  • Streaks and achievement badges
  • Sharing, friends, or a leaderboard
  • Submitting your own custom topics
  • Android version
  • A web dashboard
Under the hood

What happens every morning

The whole loop above runs without anyone touching a server. AWS wakes itself up, writes the content, saves it, and pushes it — the same pattern used by real production apps, just sized for one developer to run and maintain.
